What Evenpace 2.0 does

  • Log a dose — record each dose for one or more prescriptions (injections, pens, vials, tablets, or capsules), rotate injection sites on a body map, and jot side effects or questions for your prescriber.
  • Refills & supply — see how many pens, vials, or tablets you have left and when your next refill is due, counted from your latest fill and schedule.
  • Costs & savings — record what you paid at the pharmacy and what a savings card covered (stored by its last 4 digits only).
  • Visit & coverage packet (PDF) — generate a clean, printable summary with your medications, dose history, sites used, side effects, questions, and prior-authorization or plan-renewal date.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, iOS also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud account. Evenpace has no servers and no user accounts.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?
Turn on iCloud sync in Settings, or use Settings → Export dose log (CSV) to save a spreadsheet of your doses.

How do I cancel or manage Evenpace Pro?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open the Settings app → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Evenpace. Use Restore purchases on the Evenpace plan screen if you reinstall or get a new phone.

How do I delete everything?
In Evenpace, open Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app from your iPhone. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove Evenpace’s iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Does Evenpace give medical advice?
No. Evenpace is a personal organizer for your own records. It does not recommend doses, diagnose conditions, or interpret symptoms. Always follow your prescriber’s instructions.
