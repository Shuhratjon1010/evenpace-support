Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

Evenpace is built so your prescription and health records stay yours. There is no Evenpace account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK.

What you enter stays on your devices

  • Everything you log — medications, doses, injection sites, side effects, refills, pharmacy costs, savings-card last-four digits, coverage notes, and visit questions — is stored locally on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, iOS syncs those records through Apple’s CloudKit into your own private iCloud container (iCloud.com.evenpace.app). The developer of Evenpace cannot read your private iCloud database.

Camera & label scanning

When you scan a medication label, iOS reads the printed text on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device Vision framework. The camera image is used only to fill in the form and is neither saved to disk nor uploaded anywhere.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, Evenpace requests read-only access to your weight so it can show your latest weight and trend in your visit packet. Evenpace never writes to Apple Health and never sends HealthKit data off your device.

Widgets & notifications

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show only your next refill or visit timing and never display medication names.
  • Dose, refill, and visit reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone.

Subscriptions

Evenpace Pro purchases and renewals are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit. Evenpace receives only Apple’s cryptographic receipt confirming whether your subscription is active.

Sharing & deleting your data

  • A Visit Packet PDF or CSV export leaves your iPhone only when you choose to share, print, or save it using the iOS share sheet.
  • You can delete all data at any time from Settings → Delete all data inside the app, or by deleting the app.
