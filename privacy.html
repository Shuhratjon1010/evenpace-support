Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 (Evenpace 2.0)

Evenpace has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no tracking. Evenpace has no server. We don't collect, sell or share your data, and we can't see it.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device: medications and strengths as you typed them from your prescription, doses, injection sites, side effects, notes, visits and questions; refills and supply, what you paid and what a savings card covered, the savings card's last 4 digits (Evenpace never asks for or keeps the full card number), pharmacy and Rx details; and coverage details (insurer, plan, member ID, renewal dates and notes). Evenpace does not send it anywhere. You can erase it any time in Settings › Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.

Visit packet

The visit and coverage packet PDF is made on your iPhone from the records above; it shows only the last 4 characters of a member ID. It leaves your device only if you share or print it yourself.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional, off by default and part of Evenpace Pro. If you turn on "Read weight from Apple Health" in Settings, Evenpace reads only your weight, on your iPhone, to show it in your own visit packet. It is read-only: Evenpace never writes to Apple Health, and Evenpace never sends data from Apple Health anywhere or uses it for advertising. You can change or remove access any time in the Health app › Sharing.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is optional and off by default. If you turn on "Sync with iCloud" in Settings, your records are kept in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), which only your Apple Account can access. We, the developer, cannot access it. With sync off, nothing goes to iCloud.

Widgets

The Evenpace widget shows your next dose day and refill date. The app writes a small snapshot (dates, a schedule word such as "Weekly pen", and whether you have Pro) to a container on your iPhone that only Evenpace and its widget can read. Medication names are never included, so they never appear on your Lock Screen.

Export

"Export dose history" makes a CSV file on your iPhone (date, medication, dose, site, side effects, note). It leaves your device only if you share it through the share sheet.

Camera and photos

If you scan a pen or bottle label, the camera image is read on your iPhone to fill in the medication name, strength, form and expiry. If you choose a photo instead, it is read the same way. The image is not saved by Evenpace and is never uploaded.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Refill reminders never show the medication name.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Your control

Because your records stay on your device (or in your own private iCloud), you control them directly: you can see them in the app, export your dose history, and delete everything in Settings › Delete all my data, which also removes them from your iCloud if sync is on.

Health information

Evenpace organizes your records. It doesn't give medical advice, diagnose, or recommend doses. Talk to your prescriber about your treatment.

Children

Evenpace is not directed at children under 13.
