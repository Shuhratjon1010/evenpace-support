Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Evenpace has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log (shots, injection sites, medication name and dose as you typed it, weight, protein, water, side effects and questions) is stored only on your device. You can delete it any time in Settings > Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.

What we process

  • Meal photos, only if you allow it. When you ask for a photo protein estimate and have agreed in the app, that one photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider (Anthropic) to estimate protein. We don't store the photo, and no other part of your log is sent. You can switch this off in Settings.
  • A random install ID. Used only for a daily photo limit; not linked to you, and deleted from our server within 36 hours.
  • Purchases. Handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Health information

Evenpace is a personal journal, not a medical service. It does not diagnose, treat or recommend doses. Talk to your prescriber about your medication.

Children

Evenpace is not directed at children under 13.
